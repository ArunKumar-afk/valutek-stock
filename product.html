Agentic AI powering enterprise scale

Valucore’s intelligent infrastructure seamlessly unites workflow automation, actionable data, and strict banking governance.

01. Process Orchestration

Manage the property case lifecycle across Open, Assigned, Accepted, and In Progress stages. Valucore integrates technical property valuations and legal verification officer inputs into a single source of truth.

  • Real-time case tracking across 8 distinct lifecycle stages including Pending Approval and Report Upload.
  • Dual workflow support for simultaneous execution of Technical surveys and Legal documentation.

02. Applied Intelligence

Leverage Valutek's Agentic AI for automated data retrieval (City/Pincode based) and conversational access to property valuation insights based on real-time market trends.

  • Conversational Agentic AI enhances decision-making with location-based property intelligence.
  • Workflow-based analytics highlighting total completed, in-progress, and open property cases.

03. Absolute Governance

Enforce strict enterprise security across Super Admins, Organization Managers, Case Masters, and Verification Officers with granular Role-Based Access Control.

  • 8+ specialized roles including Region Heads and Legal Approvers with defined visibility.
  • Automated MIS reporting for operational insights, downloadable in Excel format.

04. Enterprise Scalability

A cloud-native multi-tenant platform with Flutter-based mobile support and API integration capabilities for direct banking system connectivity.

  • Domain-based configurability allowing organization-specific UI settings and calculation logic.
  • Highly configurable approval matrices and dynamic field settings to adapt to business rules.
